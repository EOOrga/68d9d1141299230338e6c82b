Daily operations in one local workspace

The Android app keeps routine business tasks organized without accounts, cloud sync, analytics, advertising identifiers, or runtime permission requests.

Appointment booking

Schedule patient visits, review upcoming bookings, and close completed appointments from the daily board.

Patient records

Save priority, last visit, and care plan notes in local app storage on the Android device.

Staff training

Assign operational courses and update progress during team reviews.

Audit notes

Every local action adds a simple audit entry for review and accountability.

Privacy-first by design

Records stay on the device until the app data is deleted or the app is uninstalled.
